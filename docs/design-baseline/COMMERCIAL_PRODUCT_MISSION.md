# Commercial Product Mission

REWIND is intended to become a real commercial software product that developers and organizations can adopt and pay for. It is not merely an academic exercise or portfolio demo.

## Product principles

- Reliability and data integrity come before feature count.
- Security and tenant isolation must be considered in future architecture.
- Installation, configuration, upgrades, diagnostics, and documentation must be practical for real users.
- Guarantees must be technically defensible and limitations explicit.
- Do not claim production readiness solely because automated tests pass.
- Do not invent customers, demand, pricing, revenue, or market validation.

## Future commercial possibilities (roadmap only)

Potential future directions may include:
- Self-hosted developer edition.
- Team and organization deployments.
- Commercial licensing and support.
- Enterprise security and audit capabilities.
- Additional transports and integrations.
- Hosted offerings if customer evidence justifies them.

These are roadmap considerations, not Phase 1 implementation requirements.

## Phase separation

1. Phase 1: trustworthy technical foundation.
2. Later phases: recovery/reconciliation capabilities and broader integrations.
3. Commercialization phases: packaging, distribution, licensing, support, and customer onboarding.

Do not add SaaS, billing, licensing, dashboards, or multi-tenancy to Phase 1 unless the owner explicitly changes scope.
